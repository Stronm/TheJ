TheJ Privacy Policy

Version 0.5 · Effective 11 October 2026 · Applies to TheJ 0.5 for Windows (Microsoft Store and standalone installer)

TheJ is made by Saanu John, an individual developer in India ("we", "us"). Contact: connetthej@gmail.com.

Summary

  • TheJ is local-first. Your boards and files stay on your computer. TheJ works fully offline unless you choose an online feature.
  • When you share or join a board online (beta), board content is end-to-end encrypted. Our relay server passes it along but cannot read it.
  • The relay does see some technical information, such as your IP address, a device ID and who is in a session. It keeps a small record of each shared board's members.
  • No ads, no analytics or usage tracking, no selling of data, and no AI features. Crash reports stay on your computer.

1. Information stored on your computer

TheJ stores the following on your computer. We do not receive it unless you send it to us.

  • Your boards (.thej files), imported images, videos, documents and 3D files, recovery and autosave files, and recent-file lists.
  • Settings and a local profile containing your display name.
  • For online collaboration: a device signing key, kept in your operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager on Windows; the system keyring on Linux). The matching public key acts as your device ID.
  • Boards you have shared online also contain a secret board key inside the .thej file. Anyone who has a copy of that file can decrypt that board's online session and ask to join it. Share the file only with people you trust.
  • Diagnostic logs and, after a crash, a crash report and memory dump (see section 4).

TheJ does not encrypt your board files on disk. Anyone or any software with access to your files may be able to read them. Backup and cloud-sync tools you use follow their own settings.

2. Online collaboration (beta)

Nothing is sent unless you choose Share, join with a code, or agree to join the live session of a shared board you open. When you do:

What is sent

Board changes, notes and comments, images and other files on the board (up to 200 MB each), image previews, your cursor position, selection and view area, item locks, your display name and colour, and your role (host, editor or viewer). This goes to the other people in the session, through our relay and, where possible, directly between computers.

Your display name is the name you set in TheJ. If you have not set one, TheJ uses your Windows account user name. Change it before sharing if you do not want others to see it.

End-to-end encryption

The content above is encrypted on your computer with the board key before it is sent. Only people who have the board key can read it. The relay cannot read board content, names in normal sessions, cursors or images.

Exception: when someone joins with a join code, their display name is sent to the relay in plain text so the host can see who is asking. After the host approves, the board key is sent to them encrypted, and everything after that is encrypted.

Our relay server

The relay is a small server we run on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects (thej-relay.connetthej.workers.dev). It passes encrypted messages between session members and enforces roles. It can see:

  • your IP address and other connection information from Cloudflare;
  • your device ID (your device's public key), the board's random ID, and join codes;
  • who is in the session, their roles, who is waiting for approval and who has been removed;
  • a code joiner's display name (see above);
  • message types, sizes and timing, and a short fingerprint (hash) of the board state used to check that copies match.

What the relay keeps:

InformationHow long
Board content, images, previews, cursors, messagesNot stored. Passed through in memory only.
Room record for each shared board: board ID, owner and current host device IDs, member device IDs and roles, removed (blocked) device IDs, a hash of the join token, room settings and an edit counterKept in Cloudflare Durable Object storage so returning members are recognised. Join codes (code and board ID)A code expires 24 hours after it is created by default and can be made single-use. Making a new code retires the old one. Expired codes are deleted when next looked up or replaced.

Each board's room is created in a Cloudflare region near the first person who opens it (for example Asia-Pacific). Cloudflare uses your approximate location from your IP address to choose the region. Cloudflare processes connection data as our hosting provider under its own privacy policy. We have not enabled request logging in the relay's configuration.

Direct (peer-to-peer) connections

To make sessions faster, TheJ tries to connect session members' computers directly using WebRTC. To set this up, it contacts public STUN servers run by Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com) and Google (stun.l.google.com), which see your IP address. Other people in the session can see your IP address when a direct connection is made. Direct traffic is encrypted. If a direct connection fails, the relay is used instead.

What other members can do

People in a session receive the board and its files and can keep their own copies, including after you leave or remove them. Hosts can approve, deny, change roles and remove people.

3. Other times TheJ goes online

  • Update check (standalone installer only). After start-up, and when you click check for updates, TheJ asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest release. If you choose to install an update, the installer is downloaded from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address and a "TheJ-Updater" user agent. Microsoft Store copies do not do this; the Store handles updates.
  • Web links and images. If you drop or paste a web image or link, TheJ downloads it from that website to show it on your board. For YouTube, Vimeo and Sketchfab links it also asks those services for a title and thumbnail. Those sites receive your IP address and the address requested.
  • Links you open. Links to GitHub, the Microsoft Store, PayPal (donations) or other sites open in your web browser, and those sites' own policies apply.

TheJ 0.5 does not use local-network discovery from its menus. An older same-network sharing tool remains in the code but is not reachable from the app's menus.

4. Diagnostics and crash reports

TheJ writes diagnostic logs on your computer. After a crash it can save a text report and a memory dump to your Desktop. These may contain file paths, technical details or fragments of your data. TheJ does not upload them. If you choose to email them to us or attach them to a GitHub issue, review them first. GitHub issues are public.

Windows and the Microsoft Store may collect their own diagnostics under Microsoft's settings and policies.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • No AI features, and your boards are not used to train AI.
  • No user accounts or sign-in.

6. How we use information

We use the information the relay handles only to run online sessions: delivering messages, checking who may join, enforcing roles, and preventing abuse. We use emails and reports you send only to provide support. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Stay offline: do not share or join online sessions. Store copies then make no network connections except for web links you add.
  • Leave a session at any time, or exit TheJ to close all connections.
  • Delete local data: delete your .thej files, recovery files and crash files. Uninstalling may not remove everything. Deleting your files does not delete copies other members already have.
  • Access or deletion on the relay: email us to ask what the relay holds about your device or board, or to delete a room record. We may ask for the board ID or device ID (stored on your computer) to find it, because we cannot read board names.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Security

We use end-to-end encryption for session content, signed device keys and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure. Online collaboration is in beta, so avoid sharing highly sensitive material.

10. Children

TheJ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy describes TheJ 0.5. We will update it when features change, for example before adding accounts, paid features or new online services, and change the date above.
